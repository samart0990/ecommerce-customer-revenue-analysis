# E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Intelligence

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project that explores online retail transactions to understand revenue trends, product performance, country-wise sales, and customer purchasing behavior.

## Business Problem
Retail businesses need insights into their best-performing products and markets, revenue trends, and customer retention to make better business decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze revenue and order performance.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Find the top 10 products and countries by revenue.
- Compare one-time and repeat customers.
- Perform RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) customer segmentation.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python — Pandas and data cleaning
- SQL — querying and aggregations
- Microsoft Excel — PivotTables and charts
- Power BI — KPI cards, charts, and dashboard filters

## Dataset
UCI Online Retail dataset, covering December 2010 to December 2011.

## Key Findings
- Approximately 393,000 cleaned transaction records analyzed.
- Approximately £8.89 million in revenue in the analyzed dataset.
- The United Kingdom contributed approximately 82% of analyzed revenue.
- November 2011 was the highest-revenue month in the dataset.
- Repeat customers represented approximately 65.6% of analyzed customers.
- RFM segmentation identified customer groups for further analysis.

## Deliverables
- Python notebook
- SQL analysis queries
- Excel analysis workbook
- Power BI dashboard
- Cleaned retail dataset

## Business Value
The analysis helps identify revenue-driving products and markets, understand customer purchasing patterns, and identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

## Data Notes
The revenue analysis uses cleaned transactions linked to customer IDs with positive quantities and unit prices. Anonymous-customer transactions, returns, and nonpositive sales lines are excluded from the revenue calculations.

The dataset ends on December 9, 2011, so December 2011 is a partial month.
